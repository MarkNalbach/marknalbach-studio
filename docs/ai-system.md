# Portfolio AI System

## Overview

The Portfolio AI Assistant allows visitors to explore projects and experience using natural language.

## Current Implementation

Questions are matched against a curated knowledge base.

Flow:

Question
→ searchPortfolioKnowledge()
→ Knowledge Match
→ Response

## Why

The goal is to demonstrate AI-oriented product thinking without relying on external APIs.

## Future Direction

Potential future versions may integrate an LLM while preserving the curated knowledge layer.
